Holy in the Highlights: Finding God in the Daily Grind

By: A Catholic Youth Blogger

Have you ever looked at pictures of saints with glowing halos and thought, "Yeah, that’s never going to be me"?

Between endless school assignments, doomscrolling on TikTok, trying to maintain a social life, and managing daily stress, trying to be "holy" can feel completely out of reach. Most of us aren't called to move to a monastery or spend eight hours a day in silent prayer. So, how are we supposed to live out God’s call to holiness when our everyday lives are so crowded and noisy?

The answer is much simpler—and much closer to home—than you might think.

The Secret: Turning Daily Tasks into Prayer

"You don't need extraordinary circumstances to become a saint; you just need to do ordinary things extraordinarily well for God." — St. Josemaria Escriva

St. Josemaria explained that our daily work, our studies, and our ordinary duties can actually become our prayer. When you sit down to study for a difficult exam, clean your bedroom, or practice a sport, you don't have to separate that from your faith. When you offer those efforts up to God with love, those moment-to-moment tasks become holy.

You don't have to escape the real world to find God; He is waiting for you right in the middle of your standard daily schedule.

The Modern Struggle: Distraction & Disconnection

Let’s be honest: doing our daily work with a good attitude isn't easy.

We live in a world designed to steal our attention. Notifications pop up every few seconds, social media algorithms keep us scrolling for hours, and peer pressure can easily push us away from prayer and Mass. It’s easy to get so caught up in online trends or personal worries that God gets pushed to the very background of our lives.

When we lose focus, we end up feeling drained, disconnected, and empty. But holiness isn't about being perfect 100% of the time—it's about choosing to turn back to God every single day.

3 Practical Ways to Practice Holiness Today

If you want to start building real virtue and grace in your life, you don't need a total life overhaul. Start with these three practical steps:

  • Offer Up Your Work (The Morning Offering): Before you start your homework or open your textbook, take five seconds to say: "Lord, I offer this study session to You. Help me do my best." Suddenly, studying isn't just a chore—it’s an act of love.
  • Set Tech Boundaries: Before scrolling through social media, pause and ask yourself if it's drawing you closer to good things or just wasting your energy. Use the THINK test before posting or replying: Is it True, Helpful, Inspiring, Necessary, or Kind?
  • Look to Modern Models: Look at Blessed Carlo Acutis, the first millennial saint. He loved computer programming, playing video games, and hanging out with friends, but he also used his tech skills to build a website cataloging Eucharistic miracles. He showed us that a young person in the digital age can be a saint without giving up being a normal teenager.

Small Steps, Eternal Impact

Holiness isn't a destination you reach overnight; it's a daily walk. Every time you choose to do your homework faithfully, encourage a friend, control your temper, or take two minutes to pray instead of checking your phone, you are answering God's call.

As St. Paul wrote in 1 Corinthians 16:14, "Let all that you do be done with love." Start small today. Offer your daily grind to God, and watch how He turns your ordinary routines into something sacred.
